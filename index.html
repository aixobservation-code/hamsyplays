HamsyPlays Uploader

HamsyPlays Uploader is a small publishing tool used by the HamsY PLaYs gaming channel. It takes short gameplay clips that the channel owner has edited and posts them to the channel owner's own TikTok account at a scheduled time, with a caption and hashtags.

The tool only posts videos the owner has created and chosen. It does not read, collect or store any other TikTok users' data.
