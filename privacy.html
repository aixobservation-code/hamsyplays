Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how HamsyPlays Uploader ("the app") handles information when the channel owner connects a TikTok account.

What we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and avatar) for that account only. It uses the token solely to upload and publish videos that the owner has selected.

What we store

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not run servers that store TikTok data, we do not sell or share data with anyone, and we do not use data for advertising.

Other users

The app does not access, collect or store information about any other TikTok users.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking it stops all posting immediately.
